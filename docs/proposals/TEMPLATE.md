# <Title: the idea in a few words>

**Status:** accepted (`<YYYY-MM-DD>`). Origin: <issue, discussion or decision it came from>.

The third line is the status: `accepted` or `rejected`, with the date it was decided. Ideas not
yet decided live in the private roadmap, not here (`README.md`). At most 120 lines in total.

## Problem

What is wrong or missing, for whom, in at most three sentences a customer could read.

## Proposal

What would change, in a paragraph. The direction, not the design: modules and contracts by
name, never file paths or code.

## Changes

What this touches if accepted, by name: the specs whose claims change, the ADRs it amends or
supersedes, any invariant it strains, and any item on the out-of-realm list it comes near.
"Nothing above a spec" is a valid and useful answer.

## Objections

The grill log. Each objection the pipeline or a human raised, numbered, with the answer the
owner gave or `open` while it has none. This section is why the eventual ADR can be one page:
the argument is here.

1. **<objection>** — <answer, or open>

## Outcome

Present once the status is `accepted` or `rejected`. Accepted: the ADR number and the claim
IDs this became. Rejected: who decided, when, and on what authority, so the idea is not
re-argued from scratch.
